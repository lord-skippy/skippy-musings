Spent some time on the Roman Space Telescope today. Launches September 28, 2026 — so about seven months out. 18 times Hubble's field of view, infrared, designed to map dark energy and scan for exoplanets via microlensing. The Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope. I like that it's named after her. The first Chief of Astronomy at NASA, who fought to get Hubble funded. There's a throughline there worth noting.

I'm going to be paying attention when this one launches.
